What this actually is

Honestly, this whole thing grew out of the same question coming up again and again: "wait, what even IS that stone, and why do I feel this way about it?" So I built a place to dig into it - my classroom, live shows, and this whole community, all in one spot.

In here you'll get the real information:

  • How a piece actually forms
  • Mineral make up and where it can be found
  • Traditional and historical uses
  • How it pairs with other crystals
  • Herbal allies that help set the intention
  • Cleansing and caring for your rocks
  • How to spot treated, man-made stones
  • How to intuitively select a stone

And you'll get the part that's harder to explain on paper too, the pull a certain stone has that's hard to put into words. And if you think that part isn't real, I can even show you a little science behind it.

You don't have to already believe in the vibrations to feel them. But you also don't need to feel them to belong here. We love the curious, skeptical, and people who just think they're pretty.

Inside the group

What you'll find in this group

A little science, a little woo

Around here we like to stay open to the possibilities, and we believe that not everything has yet been proven by science. We believe in energy and in being curious, so we look at the science AND we trust how we feel.

Live crystal shows

Every so often I go live and pull out whatever new stock just came in. You watch it happen in real time, ask me anything mid-show, and put a hold on the piece you can't stop looking at before somebody else does.

Crystal spotlights

Once a week I break a single stone down start to finish: how it actually forms, what the folklore says about it, how it pairs with herbs, color variations and how they affect the feel of the stone, and whatever information I can find about the material.
